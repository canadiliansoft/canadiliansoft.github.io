Someone shared their movie shelf with you

Cinecase is a free app for Blu-ray, DVD and 4K collections. Install it, then open the file you were sent to browse their shelf. It stays separate from your own collection.

Download on the App Store Get it on Google Play
  1. Install Cinecase.
  2. Go back to the message and tap the .cinecase file.
  3. Choose Open in Cinecase (iPhone: tap Share first if you do not see it).
