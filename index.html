Before you move

What carries over from DVD Profiler?

Supported fields include titles, production years, formats, editions, and retail barcodes. Supported multi-film box-set relationships become Collections with films inside.

Not every DVD Profiler field transfers. Special features and Easter eggs are not imported. Nested box sets and single-child profiles are not grouped the same way. Keep your original export and review the import preview before confirming.

Do I need Pro to import?

No. Imports and exports are free. The core collection experience stays free, with an optional one-time Pro purchase for extras such as batch scanning, extra libraries, and Google Drive sync.

Coming from a different app?

Cinecase also accepts CLZ XML or CSV, My Movies CSV, Letterboxd watched CSV, and generic CSV. The import preview helps you check what Cinecase recognized before adding it.

Export support and fields vary by app. Letterboxd import is for watched films, not a watchlist or full diary transfer.

Can I take my collection with me later?

Yes. Backup and export remain free, so you can keep a copy of your data. No account is required for the basic local shelf.
